What each rule actually requires

Title II

Public entities

State and local governments must meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA for web content and mobile apps. The DOJ final rule was published in April 2024 and the compliance dates were pushed by one year in April 2026.

Title III

Private businesses

No regulation sets a date. The obligation is immediate and is enforced through lawsuits and demand letters, with WCAG 2.1 AA used as the practical benchmark in settlements.

Section 504

Federal funding recipients

HHS set WCAG 2.1 AA as the standard for recipients of federal healthcare funding, with dates split by employee count and extended by one year in May 2026.
